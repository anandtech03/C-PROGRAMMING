About the Online Book Store

The Online Book Store at Rajkiya Engineering College, Banda is an academic initiative designed to streamline access to essential course materials for engineering students. Our platform provides a centralized, user-friendly space to explore, search, and procure core syllabus textbooks, technical reference manuals, and competitive examination guides. Tailored to align with our specific engineering departments, this platform ensures students have the exact learning resources required to succeed in their academic journey.
